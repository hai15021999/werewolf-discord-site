## 1. Who we are

Wolfverine ("the Bot") is a Werewolf game bot for Discord. It is a hobby project run by one independent operator ("we") and is not affiliated with Discord Inc. This policy covers the Bot and this website.

## 2. What the Bot collects

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Your Discord user ID when you join, play or act in a game (joining, night actions, votes, your role, alive or dead) | To run the game and resume it after a restart |
| Per-server game statistics linked to your user ID (games played, wins, results per role) | Statistics shown by the Bot |
| Server ID, channel ID and the server's language setting | To run games in the right place and language |
| Short-lived technical data (active game state, timers, rate-limit counters) | Operation and abuse prevention; removed automatically |
| Technical logs (errors, start-up events) | Troubleshooting; overwritten automatically |

The Bot does **not** collect or store message content, usernames or display names (names are fetched from Discord only to show them), email addresses, IP addresses or voice data. It does not use Discord's privileged intents (message content, server members, presence).

## 3. This website

This website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site, under [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 4. How we use the data

We use the data only to run games, show results and statistics, and keep the Bot working and safe. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or AI training.

## 5. Where it is stored

The Bot's data is stored on a server controlled by the operator. Only the operator has access to it and to its backups.

## 6. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Game records and statistics | Until you ask us to delete them, or until the Bot is shut down |
| Server settings | Until the server owner asks us to delete them |
| Cache and rate-limit data | Minutes to hours |
| Logs | Overwritten as the logs rotate |
| Backups | At most 14 daily copies; deleted data leaves the backups within about 14 days |

## 7. Your choices and rights

- You can stop using the Bot at any time. A server owner can remove the Bot from the server.
- You can ask us to **delete your data** (game records that include you, and your statistics). Email us with your Discord user ID. We aim to reply within 30 days. Games that are still running are deleted after they end.
- You can ask which data we hold about you.

## 8. Children

The Bot is meant for Discord users who meet Discord's minimum age in their country. We do not knowingly collect more data from younger users than described above.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date at the top. We will also mention significant changes in the Bot's help message.
